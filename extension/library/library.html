Import stickers

📥
Drop sticker files, folders, .zip or .wastickers here WebP · PNG · GIF · JPEG · AVIF — or a whole “WhatsApp Stickers” folder
  1. Open the Files app on your phone and go to
    Internal storage › Android › media › com.whatsapp › WhatsApp › Media › WhatsApp Stickers
  2. Select all stickers (or the whole folder) and share/upload them to your computer — Google Drive, Nearby Share, email to yourself, or USB cable.
  3. Drop the files (or the zip, or the folder) above. Duplicates are skipped automatically.

Older phones keep the folder at Internal storage › WhatsApp › Media › WhatsApp Stickers.

  1. Open web.whatsapp.com in this browser and link your phone.
  2. Click the StickStack icon in the toolbar → Start collecting.
  3. Open the emoji panel → Stickers and scroll through your favourites and packs (or open a chat full of stickers). They appear in the StickStack panel as WhatsApp loads them.
  4. Untick anything you don’t want and press Save.

The collector only reads images already on your screen — it never sends, clicks or changes anything in WhatsApp. WhatsApp’s built-in animated (Lottie) stickers can’t be collected.

.wastickers files from sticker-maker apps import as a pack with their title. Plain .zip files work too — folders inside become packs.

Telegram static stickers (.webp) import fine. Telegram animated (.tgs) and video (.webm) stickers aren’t supported yet.
