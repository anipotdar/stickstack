Convert your stickers into files you can post on Substack Notes (or anywhere). Animated stickers become GIFs. Everything happens on your device — nothing is uploaded.

    Where are my WhatsApp stickers?

    🤖 Android

    Tap Choose stickers, open the menu (☰) → your phone’s storage, then go to:

    Android › media › com.whatsapp › WhatsApp › Media › WhatsApp Stickers

    Select all and tap Select. Older phones: WhatsApp › Media › WhatsApp Stickers.

    🍏 iPhone

    iOS doesn’t let apps read WhatsApp’s stickers. Use a computer: open web.whatsapp.com, and either use the free StickStack Chrome extension (collects them in one go) or right-click each sticker → Save image as…, then drop the files here.

    📦 Sticker packs

    .wastickers files from sticker-maker apps and plain .zip archives work directly — just drop them in.

    Telegram animated (.tgs) and video (.webm) stickers aren’t supported yet.

    Posting on Substack

    In the Substack app or website, start a Note → tap the image button → pick your converted stickers. Notes take up to 6 images per note. On a computer, the StickStack extension adds a sticker button right inside Notes and replies.
